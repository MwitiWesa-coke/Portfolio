Book Recomendation

The 48 Laws of Power - Robert Greene

Power is never given; it's earned through strategy, perception, and discipline. Greene reveals how influence operates in everyday life, teaching readers to master emotion, timing, and the subtle art of persuasion. The book encourages awareness of hidden motives, showing that understanding power dynamics is key to both leadership and survival.

Leaders Eat Last - Simon Sinek

True leadership is'nt about authority but about service. Sinek explores how the best leaders create environments of trust and safety where people feel valued and inspired to perform their best. Drawing lessons from the military and business world alike, he shows that empathy and selflessness form the foundation of enduring success.

The Laws of Human Nature - Robert Greene

Understanding people is the highest form of intelligence. Greene dissects human behavior—our emotions, drives, and insecurities—revealing how awareness can help us navigate complex social realities. The book challenges readers to master self-control, read others deeply, and act with clarity instead of impulse.
